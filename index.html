Claude Google

Claude Google is a private, single-person tool. It lets its owner's AI assistant, running on the owner's own computer, read and manage the owner's own Google accounts: Gmail, Calendar, Drive, Docs, Sheets, Contacts and Tasks.

  • It is not a public service and is not offered to anyone else.
  • It runs locally using the open-source Google Workspace MCP server.
  • Sign-in tokens are stored only on the owner's computer.
