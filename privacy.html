Privacy policy

Claude Google · last updated 26 October 2026

Who uses this app

Claude Google is used only by its owner, with the owner's own Google accounts. It is not offered to the public.

What data it accesses

With the owner's permission, it reads and manages the owner's Gmail, Google Calendar, Google Drive, Docs, Sheets, Contacts and Tasks, so the owner's AI assistant can answer questions and carry out tasks the owner asks for.

Where data goes

  • Google sign-in tokens are stored only on the owner's own computer.
  • Data is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.
  • Data is used only to carry out the owner's own requests to their AI assistant.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time from Google Account → Third-party apps & services.

Contact

Use the support email shown on the app's Google sign-in screen.
